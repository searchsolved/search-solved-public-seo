Couch to 5k, week by week

A couch to 5k plan takes a new runner from walking to running for 30 minutes in nine weeks. Week one alternates 60 seconds of running with 90 seconds of walking.

Each week the running intervals get longer. Three sessions a week with a rest day between them is enough, and repeating a week is normal.
