Energy gels and hydration on long runs

On runs longer than 90 minutes the body needs extra carbohydrate. Take an energy gel every 30 to 45 minutes with a few mouthfuls of water.

In hot weather add electrolyte tablets to replace the salt lost in sweat. Practise in training so there are no surprises on race day.
