Runner's knee: what to do

Runner's knee is a dull ache around the kneecap that gets worse on stairs and downhill. Weak hips and quads are common causes.

Cut back on mileage, avoid steep descents and strengthen the glutes and quads with squats and side leg raises. See a physiotherapist if it lasts more than a few weeks.
