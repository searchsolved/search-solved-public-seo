Shin splints: causes and recovery

Shin splints cause pain along the inner edge of the shin bone and usually follow a sudden jump in mileage. Rest from running for one to two weeks, ice the area and keep fit with cycling or swimming.

Return gradually, check that your shoes are not worn out and add calf strengthening exercises.
