Trail shoes vs road shoes

Trail running shoes differ from road shoes in three ways: deeper lugs for grip, a rock plate to protect the foot, and a tougher upper. On hard-packed paths a road shoe is fine.

On wet grass, mud and loose stone, a trail shoe gives far more grip and confidence on descents.
