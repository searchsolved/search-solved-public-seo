What to eat before a run

Eat a meal rich in carbohydrate two to three hours before a run: porridge, toast or a bagel work well. Avoid a lot of fat and fibre, which sit heavily in the stomach.

For an early run, a banana 30 minutes beforehand is enough. Drink water through the morning instead of a large amount at once.
