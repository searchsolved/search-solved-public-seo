When to replace your running shoes

Most running shoes last between 300 and 500 miles. The midsole foam compresses long before the upper wears out, so look for creases in the foam, uneven wear on the outsole and new aches after easy runs.

Rotating two pairs gives the foam time to recover and makes both pairs last longer.
