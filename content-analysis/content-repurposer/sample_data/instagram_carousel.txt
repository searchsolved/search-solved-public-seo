**SLIDE 1:**  
**Headline:** Preserve Your Running Shoes!  
**Body:** Discover essential tips to extend the life of your running shoes and maximize your performance on each run.

---

**SLIDE 2:**  
**Headline:** Know Your Shoe Lifespan  
**Body:** Running shoes generally last between 300 to 500 miles. Proper care influences how long they actually last.

---

**SLIDE 3:**  
**Headline:** Dry Them Right  
**Body:** After a wet run, remove insoles and loosen laces. Avoid heat sources; let them dry naturally for optimal preservation.

---

**SLIDE 4:**  
**Headline:** Clean by Hand  
**Body:** Use a soft brush for dry mud. For tougher spots, a toothbrush and mild soap work wonders without damaging your shoes.

---

**SLIDE 5:**  
**Headline:** Rotate Your Pairs  
**Body:** Alternating between two pairs allows midsole foam to recover, enhancing longevity and reducing the risk of injuries.

---

**SLIDE 6:**  
**Headline:** Use Them Only for Running  
**Body:** Limit wear to running-specific activities to preserve shoes. Save an old pair for daily use to prolong mileage on your best pair.

---

**SLIDE 7:**  
**Headline:** Know When to Replace  
**Body:** Monitor mileage and watch for signs of wear, like creases and worn-out soles. New aches? It’s time for a fresh pair.

---

**SLIDE 8:**  
**Headline:** Store Them Properly  
**Body:** Keep shoes in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Heat can damage shoe materials, shortening their lifespan.

---

**SLIDE 9:**  
**Headline:** Summary of Care Tips  
**Body:** Proper drying, cleaning, rotation, and mindful usage can significantly lengthen the life of your running shoes.

---
