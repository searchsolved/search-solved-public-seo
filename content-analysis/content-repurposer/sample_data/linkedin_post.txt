**Are your running shoes holding you back?** 🏃‍♂️

Few investments yield a higher return than a quality pair of running shoes, but how well you care for them can impact their lifespan tremendously. Did you know that a good pair should last between 300 and 500 miles? 

Taking just a few moments after each run can significantly extend the life of your footwear:

**1. Dry Properly**  
After a wet run, always remove the insoles and loosen the laces. Use newspaper to absorb moisture but avoid direct heat, as it can warp the shoe’s materials.

**2. Hand-Clean Only**  
Brush off any mud and dirt with a soft bristle brush. Skip the washing machine; the harsh motions can ruin your shoes and strip away important coatings.

**3. Rotate Pairs**  
Using two pairs allows midsole foam to recover, prolonging their life. Plus, a different fit might just reduce those nagging aches!

**4. Restricted Use**  
Keep your shoes strictly for running. Everyday wear just adds unnecessary mileage!

Remember, the goal is to log your miles and watch for signs of wear. Along with comfortable cushioning, take note of any new aches—these can indicate the time for a replacement.

Ultimately, taking these simple steps not only preserves your investment but enhances your running experience.
