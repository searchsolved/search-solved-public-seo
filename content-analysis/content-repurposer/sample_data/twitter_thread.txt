🧵 Want your running shoes to last longer? It all comes down to how you care for them! Here are essential tips to keep your kicks in top shape. 👟💪  

2/7  
A good pair of running shoes can last between 300 and 500 miles, but proper maintenance can extend their lifespan.  

3/7  
**Dry them properly:**  
After wet runs, remove insoles and loosen laces.  
Stuff with newspaper and leave at room temperature.  

4/7  
Avoid heat! ☀️ Don’t use radiators or tumble dryers—excess heat hardens the midsole foam and weakens the glue. Change the newspaper every hour if shoes are soaked.  

5/7  
**Clean them by hand:**  
Brush off dry mud with a soft brush.  
For stubborn dirt, lukewarm water and mild soap is best.  

6/7  
**Rotate two pairs:**  
Give your shoes a day to recover their shape. Alternating between pairs prolongs their life and can minimize injury.  

7/7  
Keep them exclusive for running! Logging miles and replacing when worn is crucial. Proper storage away from sunlight is key.  

Invest a few minutes now for miles of enjoyment later! 🏃‍♂️✨
