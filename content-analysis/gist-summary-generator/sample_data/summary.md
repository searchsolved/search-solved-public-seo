**At a glance**
* Proper care can extend the lifespan of running shoes from 300 to 500 miles, depending on treatment.
* Dry shoes at room temperature with newspaper, avoiding heat sources that damage midsole foam and glue.
* Clean shoes by hand using mild soap and a soft brush; avoid washing machines to preserve structure.
* Rotate between two pairs of shoes to allow midsole recovery and reduce injury risk from repetitive strain.
* Log mileage and inspect shoes for wear; replace them when cushioning diminishes or new aches arise.
