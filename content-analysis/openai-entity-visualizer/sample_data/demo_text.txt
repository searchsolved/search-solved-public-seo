The six original World Marathon Majors are Tokyo, Boston, London, Berlin, Chicago and New York. The Boston Marathon is the oldest annual marathon in the world and was first run in 1897. The Berlin Marathon is known for fast times, and the London Marathon finishes on The Mall. Runners who finish all six receive the Six Star Medal from Abbott World Marathon Majors.

Eliud Kipchoge of Kenya ran a marathon distance in under two hours in Vienna in 2019. Paula Radcliffe set a womens marathon record at the London Marathon in 2003. Kipchoge has won the Berlin Marathon several times, and Radcliffe won the New York City Marathon three times. Both athletes were sponsored by Nike.

parkrun began in Bushy Park in London in 2004 and is now held every Saturday in parks around the world. Paul Sinton-Hewitt founded the event. Many new runners follow the NHS Couch to 5K plan before their first parkrun. You can record your run on Strava or with a Garmin watch.

Garmin, Polar, Coros and Suunto all make GPS running watches. Apple and Samsung sell smartwatches that track runs too. Garmin is based in Kansas, Polar and Suunto are from Finland, and Coros is based in California. Most watches upload to Strava, and Garmin has its own platform called Garmin Connect.

The Lake District in Cumbria is the home of English fell running. The Bob Graham Round covers 42 peaks, including Scafell Pike, Helvellyn and Skiddaw, and starts from the Moot Hall in Keswick. Kilian Jornet set a record for the round in 2018, and Jasmin Paris has held the womens record. The Fell Runners Association publishes the race calendar.

Nike, Adidas, Asics, Brooks, Saucony, New Balance and Hoka are among the best known running shoe brands. Asics was founded in Kobe in Japan, Adidas in Germany and Nike in Oregon. Hoka began in France and is now owned by Deckers Brands. On, a Swiss company, counts Roger Federer among its investors.
