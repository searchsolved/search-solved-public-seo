# Render Pages by Most Common Resolutions

## Features

Automatically render screenshots of your pages at the most common screen resolutions from your Google Analytics data.

- Reads screen resolution data from GA export
- Uses Pyppeteer for headless browser rendering
- Captures screenshots at your users' actual resolutions
- Great for responsive design testing and audits
